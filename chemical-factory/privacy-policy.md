## English

### 1. Summary

This app does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It contains **no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no user accounts**, and it works entirely offline.

### 2. Information We Collect

**None.** Specifically, the App does not collect or transmit:

- Account information (no registration or login is required or offered)
- Personal information such as name, e-mail address, phone number, or contacts
- Location data, photos, camera, microphone, or files
- Advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / IDFA)
- Analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports

The App performs no network communication. The Android build is not granted the `INTERNET` permission.

### 3. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is saved **only in the App's private storage on your device** and is never sent anywhere:

- Game progress (factory layout, funds, unlocked processes, research, quiz and review history)
- Settings (text size, sound, motion, vibration)

You can erase this data at any time by uninstalling the App, or by using the reset function in the App's settings screen.

### 4. Advertising

The App displays no advertisements and includes no advertising SDK.

### 5. In-App Purchases

The App contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions. No payment information is handled.

### 6. Third-Party Services

The App is built with the Unity engine. No third-party SDK included in the App collects or transmits your data.

The App is distributed through Google Play. Google, as the store operator, may collect information related to installation and distribution independently of the App; that processing is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 7. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no data at all, no personal information is collected from children either. The App is an educational game about inorganic chemistry and contains no advertising, purchases, or communication features.

### 8. Your Rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is no data for us to disclose, correct, or delete on request. All data created by the App resides on your own device and is under your control.

### 9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the App changes. Any revision takes effect when posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.
